2. What stays on your device

The App works without an account. It saves the following only on your device, using the device's local storage:

  • your scores, the place you reached and the date of each game,
  • the name you type in for the leaderboard,
  • whether sound is switched on or off.

This data is never sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time by deleting the App.

You can also play rulez in your web browser on this website. The browser version stores the same data in your browser's local storage, shows no ads and sends nothing to us. You can delete it by clearing this website's data in your browser.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”). Ads appear as an optional video you can watch for an extra heart, and as a full-screen ad every few games.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google processes data such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow it), your IP address, a rough location derived from it, the device type and operating system, and how you interact with ads.

Your choices

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. If you decline, you only see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the App.
  • App Tracking Transparency: on iOS, the App asks whether it may use your advertising identifier. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, it is not used.

Legal basis: personalised ads and the advertising identifier are only used with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Showing non-personalised ads, measuring them and preventing fraud is based on our legitimate interest in financing the App (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Transfers outside the EU: Google may process data in the United States. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and also uses the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

More information: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live. In Austria this is the Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Wien, dsb.gv.at.
