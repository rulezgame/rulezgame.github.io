How it works

Find the secret rule

A card appears. Swipe it left or right. Green means right, red means wrong. Use that to work out the rule.

Then it changes

Get five right in a row and you've cracked it, and win back a heart. Then the rule quietly changes. Rules get trickier the longer you play.

Climb through time

Points carry you upward through more than 100 places, from 500 million years ago to the edge of the universe.

Fast and fair

Quick answers score more. Every five in a row raises your bonus. Three hearts, and every cracked rule gives one back.

Play with friends

Hand your phone around. Everyone enters a name and the leaderboard keeps the best ten climbs on the device.

Works offline

No account, no sign-up, no internet needed to play. Your scores stay on your phone.

Support

How do I play?

Swipe each card left or right, or tap a side. A hidden rule decides which side is correct. Five right in a row cracks the rule; then a new one starts. You have three hearts, and a wrong answer or running out of time costs one.

I can't hear any sound.

Check that the sound button in the game isn't muted and that your volume is up. On iPhone, sound starts after your first tap.

Where is my leaderboard stored?

Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your scores. Nothing is uploaded.

Why do I see ads?

Ads keep rulez free. After a game you can choose to watch a short video for an extra heart, and every few games a full-screen ad may appear. See the privacy policy for details.

Something's broken or I have an idea.

Email us at richievalenz613@yahoo.com. Tell us your iPhone model and iOS version if something went wrong.
