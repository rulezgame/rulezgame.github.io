How to play

  1. A card appears. Send it left or right: swipe it, tap a side, or press the arrow keys.
  2. A secret rule decides which side is correct. For example: "red cards go left".
  3. A ✓ means you were right, a ✗ means you were wrong. Use that to work out the rule.
  4. Get 5 right in a row and you've cracked it: you win back a heart. Then the rule secretly changes, so find the new one.
  5. Right answers earn points, and points take you higher. Where to? Play and find out. Each new place costs more points than the last.
  6. You have 3 hearts. A wrong answer, or taking too long, costs one. Cracking a rule gives one back (never more than 3).

Tip: faster answers score more. Every 5 in a row raises your bonus (×2, ×3 …).
